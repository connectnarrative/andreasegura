SEGURA Creative Studio / Andrea Segura Face Card

Open index.html to review the static profile. It includes ES/EN copy,
four galleries, a keyboard-accessible image viewer, and a printable Face Card.
The 41 photographs are the original files from seguracategories.zip.
IMG_3345.JPG is also used on the cover and IMG_2895.jpg on the Face Card.

The inquiry form prepares a shareable brief on the device. There is no booking
inbox connected. The Face Card QR points to the owner-private portfolio URL.

To adapt this layout for another talent profile, replace their name, intro,
biography, details, photos, and image descriptions in index.html. Update the
casting subject and filename, set data-profile-url to the permanent HTTPS URL
and replace face-card-qr.png with a code generated for that URL. Confirm the
booking channel and the site's audience before distributing a card.
